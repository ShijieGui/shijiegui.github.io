01 / Concept & intention

How can a player uncover a kingdom's history through the people and images left behind?

Raven follows an outsider entering a world shaped by a lost civilisation. The raven serves as a witness to the kingdom's remains, encountering figures whose stories bring the past into the present. The central intention is to turn the discovery of history into an active experience, with understanding emerging gradually through exploration.

02 /

Interaction & narrative

Character conversations and image-based puzzles form the core of the experience. Encounters with the priest, queen, witch, and king provide narrative fragments, while assembling picture pieces makes reconstruction a physical action for the player. Bringing an image together echoes the larger task of piecing together the kingdom's history.

03 /

Visual development

Dark halls, repeated arches, and luminous stained-glass windows establish a world whose grandeur is still visible. Blue and violet tones surround sharp accents of cyan and gold, creating a contrast between the raven's dark silhouette and the architecture. The scene comparisons can show how light, framing, and geometric detail give each chamber a distinct presence while maintaining a shared visual language.

Related work — Hollow Knight
