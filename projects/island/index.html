01 / Concept & intention

How can an ordinary object make a familiar space feel unsettling?

ISLAND places the player in an unfamiliar setting where everyday objects carry fragments of a larger mystery. Its central design focus is observation: a furnished room, a photograph, or a handwritten message becomes meaningful when the player chooses to examine it. The intention is to make curiosity the starting point of the story.

02 /

Interaction & narrative

The player inspects objects, collects and uses items, and moves between connected scenes. Close-up views shift attention from the room as a whole to individual clues. This creates a rhythm of looking, acting, and reconsidering what has already been seen, allowing exploration and puzzle solving to carry the narrative together.

03 /

Visual development

Domestic interiors sit alongside open water, a mist-covered pier, and a forest. The contrast gives each location a different emotional weight while maintaining the island's sense of isolation. The room's blue curtains and circular porthole provide clear visual anchors; the outdoor scenes use mist and distance to make the spaces beyond them harder to read. The draft-to-final comparisons show how composition, colour, and environmental detail contribute to that atmosphere.

05 / Inspirations & related works

Creative connections

Inspiration — Rusty Lake

Rusty Lake ↗, including its Cube Escape series, combines surreal mysteries with atmospheric point-and-click exploration. It is a key influence on ISLAND's use of familiar objects as unsettling clues. The relevant connection is the way observation, object interaction, and narrative discovery become parts of the same experience.
