Follow the symbols, explore unexpected landscapes, and solve puzzles along the way. 01 / Concept & intention

How can opening a door feel like entering a different dream?

Wandering begins with a simple invitation: three doors, each leading somewhere unknown. The small, white-cloaked figure gives the player a consistent point of connection as the surrounding world changes. The project explores the anticipation of crossing a threshold and the surprise of finding a space that follows its own visual logic.

02 /

Interaction & narrative

Doors and symbols connect the experience's different scenes. Exploration and puzzles give the player reasons to look beyond a scene's first impression. The recurring figure and doorway motif help hold the journey together, allowing each new environment to introduce a different mood without losing the sense of one continuous adventure.

03 /

Visual development

The bright sky and ascending staircase contrast with violet water, moonlit buildings, and a quieter bedroom interior. Painterly textures give these distinct scenes a shared material quality. Against the coloured backgrounds, the character's simple silhouette stays easy to recognise. The draft-to-final comparisons can reveal how colour, architectural shapes, and character placement establish the identity of each space.
