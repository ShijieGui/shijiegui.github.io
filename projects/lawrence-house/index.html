The Three-Day Funeral at Lawrence Manor

A Gothic narrative mystery

You arrive at Lawrence Manor to attend Mr. Lawrence's funeral, expecting a quiet farewell. A courteous welcome, a house half-hidden in mist, and a few seemingly ordinary details suggest that something is wrong. Look closer, follow the clues, and discover what waits behind the manor's doors. This house is far less peaceful than it first appears.

01 / Concept & intention

How can a polite welcome make a player feel increasingly uneasy?

The funeral gives the player a clear reason to approach Lawrence Manor, while the arrival scene introduces uncertainty about the place and its inhabitants. The opening focuses on small contradictions: formal manners, an isolated house, and a host whose appearance resists explanation. Curiosity grows from the gap between a welcoming gesture and an unsettling situation.

02 /

Interaction & narrative

The supplied browser build follows the arrival sequence, the encounter with the birdcage-headed attendant, and the approach to the house. His written greetings lead into closer inspection of the entrance and mailbox. Moving between wider views and object close-ups lets an apparently ordinary detail become the next point of narrative attention.

03 /

Visual development

Layered grey mist softens the forest and obscures the house, while the attendant's silhouette remains clearly defined. The candle inside his birdcage and the warm entrance light provide small focal points within the restrained palette. The four draft/final pairs show the relationship between the surrounding landscape, the character encounter, the manor exterior, and the mailbox detail. Together, they move the visual focus from an uncertain destination toward a specific clue.

05 / Inspirations & related works

Creative connections

Related work — What Remains of Edith Finch

What Remains of Edith Finch ↗ explores family history through stories centred on individual lives and deaths. It provides a useful narrative reference for making a house a place of remembrance and discovery. For Lawrence Manor, the relevant idea is that exploring a place can bring the lives associated with it into focus.

Additional reference — The Last Door

The Last Door ↗ is a Gothic point-and-click mystery involving cryptic messages, abandoned places, and buried secrets. It offers a complementary reference for building suspense through investigation and withheld information. This comparison concerns narrative pacing and atmosphere, while Lawrence Manor retains its own hand-drawn visual identity.
