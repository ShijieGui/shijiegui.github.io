I am a 2D Game Designer and Interactive Designer currently studying the Master of Anime, Game and Interactive Design at RMIT University.

With a background in spatial and graphic design, my practice focuses on atmospheric narrative, environmental storytelling, and puzzle-based interactive experiences.

I create worlds that encourage players to slow down, observe their surroundings, and uncover stories through exploration and interaction.
