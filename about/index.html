I am a 2D Game Designer and Interactive Designer currently studying the Master of Anime, Game and Interactive Design at RMIT University. With a background in spatial and graphic design, my practice focuses on atmospheric narrative, environmental storytelling, and puzzle-based interactive experiences.

I am interested in creating game worlds that encourage players to slow down, observe their surroundings, and gradually uncover stories through exploration and interaction. Rather than relying heavily on direct exposition, I use environments, objects, visual clues, characters, and puzzle mechanics to communicate narrative information.

My projects, including ISLAND, Wandering, and Raven, explore different ways of combining visual atmosphere, mystery, and interaction. I primarily work with GDevelop, Unity, Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign, and I continue to develop my practice across game design, visual development, and interactive storytelling.
